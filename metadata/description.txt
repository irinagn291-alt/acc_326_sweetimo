Sweetimo keeps a live youth basketball box score on the sideline. Tag a jersey number, then stamp the stat. Totals add themselves.

• Tag a number on the pin rail or from Numbers, then stamp a cap
• Caps for Made two, Made three, Free throw, Rebound, Assist, Steal, Block, Foul, and Turnover
• Hold a cap to preview it, then release to count it
• Live home and away totals on the Sheet
• Undo last while the game is live
• Play tape lists every counted cap, oldest first
• Matches to add games and optionally enter a typed final score by hand
• Seal the game, then Export PDF and Share PDF from Insights
• Settings for Period length, Edit numbers, Contact, Show the intro, How a cap counts, and Reset all data
• All data stays on this phone

For parents and bench volunteers keeping a personal box score during youth games.
